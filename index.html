I started as a Business Analyst — two years of SQL, stakeholder decks, and Excel models that nobody read. That time taught me something more valuable than any PM certification: how to find the signal in noise.

Transitioning to Product felt less like a pivot and more like a natural conclusion. I had already been the person asking "why does this metric look like this?" — now I get to close the loop and determine what we build next.

Currently building growth products in Bangalore, India. Open to product roles where rigour and creativity are both valued.
